A clearer handover between people and coding tools

19 October 2026 / Build 0.24.0. Product judgment; demand, accuracy and financial viability have not been established by customer trials.

The strongest starting market

Agencies taking over unfamiliar projects, incoming maintainers and owners changing developers. An AI-assisted prototype may have been built with Claude while the next developer works in Codex. Reversium can help pass the original code, evidence, relationships and setup questions between them. It does not migrate conversations or accounts.

Why someone might use it

Free device-local inspection, a guided starting point, source-linked explanations, bounded feature maps, saved checklists and delivery/map comparisons in one review flow. Its value must be demonstrated through useful, correct handovers; AI can reproduce parts of the workflow with tools.

What is missing

Broader semantic tracing, measured accuracy, editor/repository/deployment connectors, operational recovery tests and a general-purpose sandbox. The local sandbox executes real code only for one fixed reviewed example profile. Image upload passed, but an empty reviewer queue still blocks the browser approval/publication test. Ordinary online analysis never runs a package.

Commercial route with a free analyzer

Test human-assisted handover reviews, training and supported local deployment first. Do not build billing before demand is demonstrated. Free operation still requires engineering, support, maintenance and commercial-rights review. No revenue, market fit or price is established.

A practical decision gate

  1. Test five authorized handovers across at least three unrelated codebases.
  2. Measure review time, source-verified correctness, missed requirements, misleading claims and support effort against the usual process.
  3. Seek at least three independently useful handovers and two paid pilot commitments. These are targets, not reported results.

Elevator pitch

You have the code. Now someone else needs to understand it. Perhaps you built a first version with Claude and the next developer works in Codex. Perhaps an agency is handing over a project, or you are taking over software with very little documentation. Reversium turns your code package into an evidence-backed starting point: what it appears to do, how supported features connect, which services are referenced and what still needs checking. Start free in your browser, with no installation. Your ZIP stays on your device. Give the next person the original code, a readable report, relationship diagrams and a practical checklist - so the handover begins with shared context and clear questions.
